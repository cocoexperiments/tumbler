Why Tumbler

Simple on the surface. Stubborn underneath.

Built around one idea — your PIN shouldn't be readable from the outside — and the unglamorous details that make a lock actually hold.

Shuffling digit groups

A 3×3 board where each button carries three digits. It reshuffles when the lock opens and after every wrong tap.

Shoulder-surf resistant

Tap positions say nothing about digits. Smudges, cameras and glances reveal far less than on a fixed keypad.

Stays locked

Kill the app, swipe it away, tap Home or Recents — Tumbler re-arms and brings the lock back until the right PIN is entered.

Brute-force lockout

After five wrong taps, escalating lockouts kick in — 30 seconds, then longer, up to 15 minutes.

Encrypted on-device

Your PIN lives in encrypted storage on your phone. No accounts, no servers, no analytics, no network calls.

Looks like it belongs

A calm dark Material 3 design with a clock, a clear prompt and gentle haptics. Boots with your phone, ready after every restart.

Fingerprint or PIN — your choice

Turn on fingerprint unlock and touch the sensor to get in, or just use the shuffling keypad. The PIN is always there as a fallback, and the first unlock after a restart always asks for it. Off by default.

How it works

Tap the button that holds your digit.

Your PIN is four digits. You never type digits — you pick which group contains the next one.

  1. Create a 4-digit PIN

    Set it once, confirm it, done.

  2. Wake your phone

    The lock appears with a freshly shuffled board.

  3. Tap the group with your digit

    Each digit shows up on at least two buttons, so there's always more than one right answer.

  4. Wrong tap? It reshuffles.

    The sequence resets and the board is redealt from scratch.

  5. Or just touch the sensor

    With fingerprint unlock turned on, the system prompt appears first. Tap “Use PIN” any time to switch to the keypad.

Security, plainly

Built with honest trade-offs.

Android doesn't let any app replace the system lock. Tumbler is explicit about what it does and doesn't do.

Good to know: Tumbler sits above your phone's lock as an extra PIN layer. For a single unlock, set the system lock to None or Swipe — the app walks you through it, and explains the trade-off. Fingerprint unlock is the exception: Android only enrols fingerprints when the phone has its own screen lock, so use one or the other.
  • No network access to your PIN. Stored locally in encrypted preferences.
  • Optional lock guard. An Accessibility service that only notices which window is in front — it can't read your screen.
  • Fingerprint is optional. Uses Android's own biometric prompt, so Tumbler never sees your fingerprint. The PIN always works, and is required after a restart.
  • Phone calls still work. The guard lets calls through.
  • Open source. Read every line on GitHub.
  • Escape hatch. Stuck? Boot into Safe mode and uninstall.

How to install
  1. Download the APK on your phone.
  2. Open it and allow “Install unknown apps” for your browser or file manager if asked.
  3. Open Tumbler, create your PIN, and grant the permissions.
  4. Turn on “Enable lock screen”, then press the power button twice to test.
  5. Optional: turn on “Unlock with fingerprint” (needs a fingerprint enrolled in Android settings).
Accessibility switch greyed out?

On Android 13+, a sideloaded app needs one extra step: App info → ⋮ menu → Allow restricted settings. Then enable “Tumbler lock guard”.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does it replace my system lock?

No — Android doesn't allow that for any app. Tumbler appears on top of the system lock. To avoid unlocking twice, set the system lock to None or Swipe; the app explains the trade-off.

Can I unlock with my fingerprint?

Yes, if you turn it on in the app. You can then touch the sensor or use the PIN keypad — either works. Android only enrols fingerprints when the phone has its own screen lock, so fingerprint unlock can't be combined with setting the system lock to None. After a restart, the PIN is needed first.

Can someone guess my PIN by tapping?

Each tap narrows a digit to three possibilities and the board reshuffles after any mistake. After five wrong taps, Tumbler adds escalating lockouts of up to 15 minutes.

Is anything sent off my device?

No. There are no accounts or analytics, and your PIN is stored in encrypted storage on the phone.

What if I forget my PIN?

Reboot into Safe mode, where third-party apps are disabled, then clear or uninstall Tumbler.

Is it on Google Play?

Not yet. Full-screen lock apps face Play restrictions, so for now Tumbler is distributed as an APK on GitHub.
